# Wellfound

1. Confirm a candidate session and collect jobs from the Wellfound jobs feed. Track unique job IDs as the feed scrolls, then open the exact listing.
2. Check freshness, match, full description and eligibility. Check for an existing application before clicking Apply.
3. Open the native application. A company-site redirect is an external handoff. A sign-up or login prompt means the candidate session needs attention. Respect location and experience restrictions shown in the application dialog.
4. Prepare the role-specific pitch and verify that the native form retained it. Route unsupported or blocked dialogs to review where indicated. Submit through the verified control within the application form.
5. Reopen the exact Wellfound listing and require persisted **Applied** evidence on that job. An unverified submission remains `submission_unconfirmed`.

**Verify** native pitch retention, a redirect, a location or experience block, and the persisted state on the exact listing. See the [Wellfound adapter](../../naukri_agent/platforms/wellfound.py).
